DAY 1

A strange new land, ripe with untold wonders and mysteries… What awaits Gack as he takes his first steps into the world of FSFables? What curious souls will we encounter (or trap), and what secrets will we unearth? Only time will tell what fate has in vine for Gack and us… But first, let's hope we can finally put down some roots and find ourselves a cosy little pumpkin patch to call home. After all, even the most mischievous of pumpkins needs somewhere to rest its vines!

When we arrived this foreign land for the first time, Gack’s excitement seems to have taken over and overstimulated his mind, at times you would find him staying still just to let his actions catch up with his thoughts… I wonder if this is what the other realm called ‘lagging’…

Upon the astonishingly speedy discovery of one of our kins, he was overjoyed to collect another fellow pumpkin, though ignoring the ritual candles and sigils around it might not be his best move… Turns out this pumpkin has almost as much Halloween whimsy as Gack, just not as much fight.

We met a new friend! Gack was able to charm him with charcoal and he quickly moved in with us. Although Gack almost exposed his pumpkin-self on the first night, we’ll get along just fine. Oh wait.. Gack kicked him out and made him live on the hill. Guess we’re neighbors now! As a house warming gift, we gave him CROISSANT SHAPED MAGGOTS!!

A condescending alchemist elf came looking for trades with his zombie horse.. as fellow halloween creatures we would have loved to welcome the undead steed into our little family! But Gack's simple pumpkin bribery is sadly not what he was looking for. Even worse, he had the audacity to call the great pumpkin warlock a “simple-minded creature” before taking his leave! Hmph! Such arrogance! We Crabkin shall not forget this slight… Perhaps one day we'll have a few tricks up our vines for him. After all, not everyone deserves a treat!

Our first pumpkin patch in this land! Gack had finally planted the seeds of what shall surely become a magnificent pumpkin empire! A humble beginning, perhaps, but every great pumpkin start somewhere. Soon, there shall be pumpkins aplenty! Pumpkins for pies, pumpkins for homes, pumpkins for… other purposes. Hehehe…

Hahaha!! It was HILARIOUS to see Gack the undead have a taste of death every now and then, that Hunter Familiar gave us Crabkins a good laugh, maybe we should repay with pumpkin pie someday when they are not hostile.

Gack adobpted a new pet chicken - Tyler Dirton, and we are truly worried for her.. Us Crabkins had seen Gack's tricks and pranks, but Tyler... It might even be Gack's most cruel act we have witnessed - countless unhatched children cracked in the name of gambling, right in front of the mother hen's eyes. May those poor souls find their pumpkins to rest in pieces.

We ventured forth with three new friends: Va’len, Harris, and Rollo (who is from what Gack called the ‘buy-you’?) and stumbled upon a mysterious castle… only to find ourselves face-to-face with a fearsome dragon! Claws, flames, and a whole lot of screaming later, we somehow escaped with our vines intact! Phew! We do enjoy a little danger, but we'd rather not end up as roasted pumpkin soup!

CANDYLAND!! What a spook-tacular discovery! Chocolate walls, waffle ceilings, and the most whimsical creatures roaming about! Oh we could hardly contain our excitement we almost wandered off from the party! A place where tricks and treats go hand in hand, we must return with more pumpkins to hold all the sweets. I wonder would anyone notice a few missing bites...?

Though still strangers to this foreign land, Gack has already learned much, yet perhaps only scratched the surface. As his powers (and obsessions with pumpkin pie bribery) grow, new acquaintances cross his path, something stirs on the horizon. Adventure awaits… but first, let's hope Gack finishes building us that pumpkin abode before our journey truly begins... we need a roof...
