DAY 3

We were jolted awake today to the sound of Gack's excitement... Apparently while we were fast asleep, he'd gone off on a little adventure of his own! Before our brains could begin to function, he was already eagerly showing us all sorts of new discoveries! Something about... treasures? New tricks? Strange places? Truth be told we were still half asleep and his words were going in one vine and out the other... But whatever he'd been up to, it certainly seemed exciting! But maybe just... five more minutes... plz.. zzz...

We stumbled upon a mysterious vacant tower, and goodness the TREASURES we found!! So many things he'd been searching for, but best of all - WE FOUND TRAPPED PRESENTS!! HEHEHE we can already think of a few delightful uses for these~ And would you belive it, ANOTHER tower just around the corner?! Looks like today is gonna be quite the fruitful treasure hunt, assuming we survive it...

All we wanted was a little quartz from the Nether, Gack built us a portal and off we went... Except WHERE IN THE GOURD ARE WE?? This is not the familiar Nether we knew, we found ourselves surrounded by dazzling purple walls! The pretty sight was but a distraction from the ominously threatening monsters lurking around every corner... Ooo sending shivers down our vines, and not the good Halloween kind... We tread carefully, with Gack hanging on by his last breath we somehow scrambled back through the portal in one piece. Phew...

So um... our house is.. still under construction... Well funny enough it does look like a pumpkin.. just maybe a little bit rotten and cracked open... but I'm sure Gack has his grand plan with finessing it, right..? As a spirit born from pumpkin he's not gonna just leave it like this RIGHT?! And what does he mean when he said it looks like "last year's pumpkin" sigh...

Gack's egg gambling had finally hatched something unexpected - A COCKATRICE!!! At first he seemed a little shocked by his sudden fatherhood, perhaps even a tad mean to the poor thing... But before long he named her Little Gacky! Though she grew up so fast she is now almost as big as Gack himself, we can hardly call her 'Little'. Watching Gack teach her the ways of life, care for her, growing attached... By the end of the day, he was even worried for Little Gacky's safety and well-being. Aww looks like someone has finally hatched a soft spot in his heart, this is sweeter than candy corns~

We don't often venture underwater since we don't want to be soggy pumpkins, but I will say the ocean was quite a sight, filled with fascinating creatures and ruins~ Haa such a soothing time- wait Wait WAIT GOOD GOURD THAT IS A GIANT SERPENT!!!! We held onto our vines and seeds while Gack somehow managed to slay the beast! We nearly had our souls scared right out of our pumpkins! Perhaps we should stick to dry land where we belong... and where the sushi aren't trying to EAT US!

GASP I can't belive we found an underwater palace. Its towering architecture was breathtaking yet strangely intimidating, as if we weren't meant to be there. And we certainly weren't welcome. The halls were dark and gloomy, crawling with creatures even stronger than the sea serpent! Before we could regret our intrusion, Gack was overwhelmed by trident-weilding foes, and this time... even his trusty undying totem couldn't save him. I reaaallly don't think pumpkin belongs in the ocean...

Our treasures...all gone. Sitting in the depths of the cursed ocean. We desperately sough help from Harris and Corvin for a retrieval mission! But in all the panic, it seems like Gack has forgotten one teeeny tiny detail...HE'S SUPPOSED TO HIDE HIS UNDEAD IDENTITY! Had Corvin not suggested taking boats, Gack might've strolled along the ocean floor without a care for drowning, revealing his secret! Thank gourd...

Just when we though things couldn't get any worse, our journey back seemed to be far more treacherous than before... Another sea serpent came to avenge its fallen kin, fiercer than the last two, and Gack was certainly its target. But the REAL nightmare awaited us back at the palace, even more foes had gathered within, way beyond anything Gack had anticipated! Death was lurking around every corner, and escape felt almost impossible.

If not for Harris and Corvin, we would've lost Gack countless times over. They fought bravely by our side, and nearly sacrificed their own lives to get us out alive! We shall never forget what they've done for us. One day we will repay their kindness, even if it takes every last seed we've got!

After all that Corvin had done for us, it was finally OUR turn to return the favour! To hunt down the possessed paladin Kaelen and the fearsome monster Cumulonimbus. Gack wasted no time packing his bags and off we went! There were a few too many close calls with death for our liking… But amidst the danger came laughter, silly jokes, and even some heartfelt conversations. Somewhere along the way, it seems these two have grown quite fond of each other! And in the end, EVIL WAS VANQUISHED!!

Our day was far from over, Hyr joined the party and off we went to see what other wonders (and horrors) this land had in store for us. From towering Cyclops to majestic Griffin, and even a mysterious giant clock fortress that refused to let us in! Just how many secrets is this land hiding?! Though with each adventure the foes we encountered grow ever more fearsome, we've noticed Gack's powers are growing stronger too! He is slowly but surely becoming quite the formidable warlock!

As Gack and Hyr grew closer, it seems Gack found a little more courage to open up about the secret he carries. Perhaps knowing Hyr is an eldritch k-night made it easier to share a part of himself he'd kept hidden from the others. He spoke of his origins, born from a pumpkin as the Spirit of Halloween, brought into this world by an eldritch deity for a purpose… and with no certainty that he'd live beyond the season. We've always known this truth, but hearing Gack speak of it so openly made our vines ache. For all the good he wishes to bring to this world, he wishes to stay in it too. And Gack is far too good a soul to lose. If there's anything we can do to help him keep living, we'll do whatever it takes.

Who knew so much could happen in just one day?! With countless near-death experiences and lurking threats came wonderful discoveries and unexpected friendships, we've certainly been through A LOT. The more we explore, the more we realise how little we know about this land, and the secrets yet to be revealed. Our vines might be aching but our souls are fuller than ever! Now finally it's time to rest in the comfort of our cozy home- WAIT. WE STILL HAVEN'T FINISHED OUR HOUSE?!?! GACK!!!
