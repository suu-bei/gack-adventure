DAY 2

Brrrr... We woke up to find the whole land covered in a fluffy blanket of snow! A pretty sight indeed, if only we have a roof over our heads... Our vines are slowly freezing... For once, maybe we should trade a little adventure for a warm and cozy home! Let's get to work!

After a little tidying and sorting through our ever-growing piles of treasures (...or questionable belongings), Gack told us about his grand vision -- a GIANT PUMPKIN ABODE!! With cakes as walls!! We weren't sure how sturdy that might be, but at least it would be our sweet home teehee~ Though I can forsee a sliiiiight problem, we CANNOT accidentally tear down the house for a midnight snack craving... We watched him climb up and down carefully laying down the foundation, one cake at a time our home is definitely coming together! ...we think...

GREAT NEWS! Our dear Tyler had given birth to QUADRUPLETS! Four tiny fluffy chicks! After all the shell-crushing horrors we had witnessed, we could not be more proud of our feathery friend~ As our little family grows, so do our hearts (and our collection of potential souls). May these little ones grow big and strong, and may Gack PLEASE keep his gambling hands away from more eggs!

This might be Gack's best discovery - he found out how to craft Scarecrow Armour!! One with a Jack-O-Lantern head too!! Finally some proper pumpkin fashion, slay, we Crabkin wholeheartedly approve. And just as we're jumping around with excitment, Harris happened to pass by to witness the goodness hehe~ Gack immediately crafted the headpiece to show off his new glamour, such proud little pumpkin~ Now we just need enough materials for the rest of the outfit, soon he'll be looking gourd-geous from head to toe!

Just as our pumpkin abode was finally taking shape, we ran out of cake... Looks like another trip to candyland is in order before we can have a roof! But to venture back on our own, Gack needed a few more tricks up his sleeves, so we went to visit Va'len, who kindly helped Gack get a better grasp of his nature magic. With some proper guidance, soon enough he would be ready to branch out on his own~ (haha get it? Branch out? Nature magic? Pumpkins? ...ahem.)

Ok I take it back, maybe Gack still needs some more practice on these new found powers.. He's never failed to keep us on our vines when he decided to conjure a rocky aerial mount that was just slightly out of his control... We almost lost our stems and seeds with all the flying we did (falling to be exact), holding onto his hood for dear life- or whatever is left in us.

GACK!! WE SWEAR TO THE DEVILS, IF YOU DARE HURT TYLER AND HER CHILDREN YOU WILL FIND OUR VINES AROUND YOUR NECK TONIGHT! HOW DARE YOU PRACTICE MAGIC IN THAT DIRECTION, THAT'S THEIR HOME!! Sleep with one eye socket open...

Our little journey took quite the detour... straight into a mage tower filled with all sorts of spine-chilling creatures! Gack fought bravely but still met his doom a few times (I've lost count..), and somehow kept coming back for more! After many attempts, he finally conquered the tower and claimed its treasures. Haha for someone whose spirit was given life out of pumpkin, Gack's soul sure is a strong and stubborn one to stay alive~

Somehow Gack stumbled upon a field absolutely SWARMING with pigs, a place he so lovingly named "Oink Heaven"... But beneath all the potential future pork chops, we discovered a mysterious underground entrance... and a villager named Keven? He seems awfully ordinary AND suspicious at the same time... Could he be behind this endless supply of oinks? What is he plotting down there?! Something smell fishy... or should we say porky? :)

After an eventful day, we enjoyed a quiet flight home with our 'trusty' flying rock (maybe we should name it...), a little time to rest our weary vines. A good night's rest is certainly in order! ...Wait, hold on a second... WE STILL DON'T HAVE A PROPER ROOF!! Oh Gack, you cleared towers, cheated death, collected treasures, yet finshing our home may just be your greatest challenge... Ah well, there's always tomorrow. Sweet dreams, and hopefully no snow fall on our pumpkin heads tonight!
